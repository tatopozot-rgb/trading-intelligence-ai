## 3. Architecture in one paragraph

`detect_regime` -> `StrategyRouter` (regime -> strategy or NO_TRADE) -> strategy proposal
-> `RiskEngine.validate_order` -> sized MARKET order -> `PaperAdapter` (next-bar-open
fills, 5 bps slippage, 0.1% fee) -> protective STOP -> exits -> P&L. Long-only Binance
Spot. `PaperTradingRunner` (`trading_intelligence/execution/paper_runner.py`) wires it:
reservations in the RiskEngine from approval to fill; fail-closed `reconcile()`; restart
recovery; a pre-fill veto (halts, `RiskEngine.validate_fill`, forced `RISK_CLOCK_ERROR`);
chronology guard (stale/duplicate bars ignored); one equity snapshot per timestamp;
protective STOP submitted before risk bookkeeping.
